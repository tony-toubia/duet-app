# Terms of Service: brief for counsel

**Status: NEEDS COUNSEL.** Duet has no Terms of Service yet, and nothing in the
app or website links to one. This is a factual brief so counsel can draft them.
It deliberately contains no legal wording. When the Terms exist:

- publish them at `getduet.app/terms`
- link them from the website footer and the sign-up screen (app and web)
- add the URL to the App Store and Google Play listings

On iOS, Apple's standard EULA applies until a custom one is supplied in App
Store Connect.

## What Duet is (facts to describe)

- An always-on voice line between two people (1:1 rooms) or a small group
  (party rooms, up to 6), on iOS, Android and the web.
- Audio:
  - Goes directly between devices when possible, otherwise through Duet's
    own relay server.
  - Is encrypted in transit and never recorded or stored.
- Accounts:
  - Apple, Google or email sign-in, or a guest (anonymous) account.
  - Guest rooms are time-limited unless the guest watches an ad or
    creates an account.
- Free, ad-supported:
  - AdMob on mobile: native, full-screen and rewarded ads.
  - AdSense on the web.
  - A rewarded ad gives one hour without full-screen ads.
- Content Hub: links to third-party content (Spotify, podcasts via
  PodcastIndex, sports via TheSportsDB). Duet doesn't host that content.
- Social features: friends, friend codes, invitations, recent connections,
  presence (online/last seen, friends only).
- Marketing email (Resend) and push notifications, with opt-out.

## Points the Terms need to cover (decisions for counsel)

1. **Eligibility.**
   - The app enforces 13+ with a neutral age gate.
   - Counsel to confirm 13 for all markets (EU digital-consent ages go up
     to 16).
   - Decide whether to require parental consent for under-16s/18s.
   - Decide whether to raise the minimum.
2. **Acceptable use.**
   - Should match the in-app report reasons: harassment or bullying, hate
     speech, sexual content, spam or scams, under-13 users, self-harm or
     danger, other.
   - Also cover recording others without consent: Duet doesn't record, but
     users could with other tools.
   - Also cover illegal use and attacks on the service.
3. **Moderation.**
   - Users can block and report. Moderators review reports in the admin
     panel and can dismiss, mark warned, or suspend (disable sign-in).
   - The Terms should reserve the right to suspend or terminate, and say
     how appeals work (contact: hello@getduet.app).
4. **No recording / no monitoring.**
   - Duet can't review audio, so moderation relies on reports.
5. **Ads and rewarded ads.**
   - Rewarded-ad benefits (1 hour without full-screen ads; extending guest
     time) are not purchases and may change.
6. **Third-party services and links.**
   - Content Hub links, app deep links (Spotify, YouTube, Maps, Waze), and
     sign-in providers.
7. **Account deletion.**
   - In-app (Profile → Delete Account), immediate.
   - Also by email via getduet.app/delete.
   - Safety reports are retained after deletion (see the privacy policy
     NEEDS COUNSEL note on retention).
8. **Availability.**
   - Rooms may be cleaned up when nobody is connected.
   - Connectivity depends on networks (relay used when direct fails).
   - No uptime guarantee.
9. **Intellectual property.**
   - Duet is proprietary (see LICENSE).
   - User-provided content is limited to display name, profile photo and
     report text.
10. **Liability, disclaimers, governing law, dispute resolution, changes
    to the Terms, and the legal entity name and address.**
    - The privacy policy also lacks the entity; see its NEEDS COUNSEL notes.
11. **Platform terms.**
    - Apple's minimum EULA terms if a custom EULA is used.
    - Google Play's requirements for user-generated-content apps:
      - in-app reporting ✔
      - blocking ✔
      - acceptable-use policy ✘ (this document)
